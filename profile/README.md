## What Notch is

Notch is a desktop **screen recording and video editing app** for **macOS 11+** and **Windows 10+**. It covers the whole path from capture to a finished video: record the screen, mic, system audio and camera, then trim, zoom, annotate, caption and export — without moving the footage into a separate editor and without uploading anything anywhere.

It is built for people who make **software tutorials, course lessons, product demos, DevRel content, and localized video** — where the recording has to look deliberate, not like a raw capture.

## What it does

### Record & zoom
- Capture a **screen or a single window** with mic, system audio and camera in one setup.
- **Auto-zoom that follows the action** — Notch detects where you pause and zooms in automatically, with smooth transitions and zero keyframing.
- **Cursor, click and keystroke highlights** so every interaction is visible on screen.
- **Audio-only and camera-only** recording for voice-overs and talking-head clips.

### Record in parts, export as one
- **Multi-take recording**: shoot a lesson section by section instead of restarting a 10-minute take because of one stumble.
- Reorder parts, drop the ones you don't need, cut mistakes out of any clip, add transitions between parts, and export a single video.

### Style & polish
- **50+ gradients, mesh backgrounds and custom wallpapers**, plus shadow, blur, rounded corners and padding.
- **Style presets** — save a visual setup once and apply it to every future recording in one click, so a whole course stays on brand.
- **Annotations**: text, arrows, shapes and blur zones, with enter/exit animations and precise timing on the timeline.
- **Webcam overlay** you control — 9 positions, custom size, mirror toggle, shown or hidden anywhere on the timeline.

### Captions & localization
- Add **caption tracks in multiple languages** and ship a localized version per audience.
- Export **burned-in subtitles** or a standalone **SRT file** for YouTube, Vimeo or your LMS.

### Edit & export
- Trim dead time, cut mistakes with blur, darken or hard-cut transitions, and change speed from 0.25x to 2x.
- Export **MP4 up to 4K** (H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1) or a compact **GIF** — handy for docs, pull requests and Slack.
